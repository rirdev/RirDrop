Scan to Connect Phone

Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network and scan:

http://192.168.1.102:53318
1. Phone and PC must be on same Wi-Fi (or phone hotspot).
2. Open phone camera or browser and open the link.
3. When prompted on PC, click Accept to start streaming!
